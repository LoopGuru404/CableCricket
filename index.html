Cable Cricket Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 1, 2026

Introduction

Cable Cricket ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

Cable Cricket does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

Local App Preferences

  • The App stores your theme preference (light or dark mode) locally on your device using SharedPreferences.
  • This preference never leaves your device and is not sent to our servers or any third party.

Offline Use

All core features — color code lookups, reference charts, and field calculators — work without an internet connection.

The production Android app does not require network access for normal use. Fonts and other assets used by the App are bundled inside the application.

How We Use Information

We do not collect personal data. Local preferences are used only to restore your chosen theme the next time you open the App.

Data Storage and Security

  • App preferences are stored locally on your device
  • We do not operate servers that store user data
  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not sell or share user data with third parties

Third-Party Services

The App does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other third-party services that collect user data.

Typography uses the Poppins font family bundled within the App. Fonts are not downloaded from external servers at runtime.

Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date in the App and on our website.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. You can reset local app preferences by clearing the App's storage in your device settings.
